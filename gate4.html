two useful negatives

Scale and radius did not become magic.

Point-only active probes already reach full rank; multiscale improves identity only modestly (.761 → .779). Flattening every dendritic radius to the real-cell median also keeps full rank and actually increases the worst singular value.

Mechanism lesson. In this passive real-morphology model, moving the stimulation address removes an algebraic ambiguity: the soma-response Jacobian becomes full-rank. But several directions are still tiny at the soma. The bottleneck changes from missing directions to attenuated directions. That supports neither central dendritic reconstruction nor biological tomography. It gives the next test a sharp target: does the already-established HUMAN NMDA feedback in Operaattori selectively rescue those weak directions?
