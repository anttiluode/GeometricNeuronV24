# References and prior art

V24 sits inside occupied mathematics. The current contribution is an executable recombination and a measured boundary, not the invention of multiresolution analysis, single-pixel imaging, adjoints, or active experimental design.

- S. G. Mallat, “A Theory for Multiresolution Signal Decomposition: The Wavelet Representation,” *IEEE TPAMI* 11(7), 674-693 (1989). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.192463). The dyadic box lenses are Haar scaling-function measurements; nested approximation spaces explain their rank redundancy.
- M. F. Duarte et al., “Single-Pixel Imaging via Compressive Sampling,” *IEEE Signal Processing Magazine* 25(2), 83-91 (2008). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/MSP.2007.914730). A scalar detector plus designed spatial masks is established single-pixel imaging; random/global masks are mandatory attackers here.
- C. Bösch, Y. Gediz, and H. Türeci, “Unifying Physical Backpropagation,” arXiv:2608.11585v1 (2026). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11585). Conditions under which a physical forward system can also generate an adjoint field.
- [GeometricNeuronV21](https://github.com/anttiluode/GeometricNeuronV21). Autopsy of the original ECG loop: quantization, aliasing staircase, readout taps, and variance-controller regime map.
- [GeometricNeuronV22](https://github.com/anttiluode/GeometricNeuronV22). Reset toward writable spatial state, selective receivers, and externally testable claims.
- [GeometricNeuronV23](https://github.com/anttiluode/GeometricNeuronV23). Geometry plus local hidden state, receiver-relative outputs, and structured-versus-shuffled address obligations.
- [GeometricNeuron_V20](https://github.com/anttiluode/GeometricNeuron_V20). Curated earlier operator line and its standing kill ledger.

Relevant classical search terms for extension work: linear/nonlinear observability, observability Gramian, sensor placement, Bayesian experimental design, active sensing, system identification, compressed sensing, Haar multiresolution analysis, and optimal input design.
